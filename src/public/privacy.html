Privacy Policy

DashHack · AT&T Hackathon Junior 2026
Last updated: 10 November 2026

The event organizers operate this DashHack deployment and Discord application only for the private AT&T Hackathon Junior 2026 event: supporting mentors, organizers and invited student teams. We do not use collected information for other events, advertising, profiling, sale or AI training.

Information handled

  • Discord commands: your Discord user ID and display name, the configured server/channel IDs, interaction ID and time, your submitted help request, and the team associated with that channel. The app receives the commands and forms you submit; it does not read channel message history or direct messages.
  • Event records: team and project details, mentor names and attribution, notes, help requests and Startup Matrix answers. A help request includes the submitting Discord display name; a private action log records the verified account ID.
  • Access and operation: essential session cookies and temporary link/session records, plus technical request information handled by the app or hosting provider for delivery, security and rate limiting. Discord form sessions expire after two hours. The organizer’s registration token is sent to Discord once and is not stored by DashHack.
  • Optional features: GitHub account/repository information and invitation audit records when you use repository joining; photos, author names and post text when you submit to the social wall. External social posts may be imported when organizers enable collection.

Why we use it

We use this information to associate submissions with the correct team, coordinate mentor help, maintain project answers, support event review/exports, verify access and prevent abuse. The private Discord action log is visible to users logged into the event, including authorized mentors and organizers. The application does not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies.

Who can see it

Mentors and organizers use the event records. Team details, matrix answers and notes are also readable by anyone who can open the event URL; “private event” describes the intended audience, not a confidentiality guarantee. Social wall posts approved for display can appear on the event wall. Exported workbooks can be shared by organizers for this event’s review.

Railway hosts the application and stored data; this deployment runs in the United States (San Francisco region). Discord processes commands and replies under its own Privacy Policy. GitHub and enabled social services process their respective features under their own policies. We do not send Discord submissions to unrelated services or use them outside this event. Access to technical administration and backups is restricted to the people operating this deployment.

Retention and removal

Event records remain available for the event and its organizer follow-up, then organizers remove or anonymize them when no longer needed. Temporary form sessions expire after two hours; expired records and Discord action logs older than seven days are cleaned up when new actions are processed. Expiry does not itself erase notes or matrix answers. Backup and exported copies require separate organizer cleanup; they are not automatically erased by session expiry.

To request access, correction or deletion, contact an event organizer or server administrator directly in the event’s Discord server, or speak to an organizer in person. Identify your Discord account and team so the organizer can locate the records and verify the request. Do not send your password or token. Organizers can remove personal attribution or records and arrange cleanup of their backup/export copies. Information already copied by other viewers cannot be recalled by the application.

Your choice

You can stop using the commands and ask a mentor for help directly. Submit only information needed for the event; avoid personal, confidential or sensitive information in team answers and help requests. Terms of Service describe permitted use.
