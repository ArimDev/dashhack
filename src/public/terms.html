This DashHack deployment and its Discord application are an internal tool for the private AT&T Hackathon Junior 2026 event. They are operated by the event organizers for organizers, mentors and invited student teams. They are not offered for general public use, unrelated Discord servers or other events.

What the application does

DashHack helps mentors and organizers support teams, manage help requests and record project information. In the configured team channels, /mentor submits a help request and /matice opens that team’s Startup Matrix form, and /github provides its GitHub sign-in link. New help requests can also be announced to the event’s mentor channel. Access follows the event’s channel and application permissions.
